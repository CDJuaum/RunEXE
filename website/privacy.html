Privacy by default

Privacy & cookies

The RunEXE website is intentionally simple. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, account systems, contact forms, or non-essential browser storage.

Who operates this site

The RunEXE project maintainers operate this website. Privacy questions or requests can be raised through the RunEXE GitHub repository's issue/support channel.

Cookies and local storage

RunEXE does not intentionally set application cookies or use local storage for visitor identifiers, preferences, analytics, or advertising. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security mechanisms while delivering the site. Because there are no optional advertising, analytics, or profiling cookies, there is no optional-cookie consent banner to dismiss.

Hosting and network data

The site is delivered through Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP addresses, request metadata, and security signals to deliver and protect the service. This processing is limited to operating and securing the website and is based on the legitimate interest in providing a reliable, abuse-resistant service. RunEXE does not add its own analytics layer on top of that traffic.

External links and downloads

Download, source-code, issue, and license links lead to GitHub. When you follow an external link, that provider's privacy terms and storage practices apply.

RunEXE application data

The RunEXE desktop application stores recent-app information, launch presets, backups, and managed environments locally on your Linux machine. This website does not receive that local application data.

Your data-protection rights

Where the GDPR or another applicable data-protection law applies, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Because this website has no accounts, analytics profiles, or contact-form database, the site itself generally has no visitor profile to retrieve or delete.

Changes

If optional analytics, forms, advertising, or other data-collecting features are added later, this notice and the site's consent controls must be updated before those features are enabled where consent is required.
